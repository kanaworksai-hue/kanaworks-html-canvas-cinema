How to use

Upload an image or video, place the fan, then drag the curtain to watch the screen become a physical cinema surface.

  1. 1Tap Upload Media to place your own image or video on the curtain.
  2. 2Use the fan controls to change wind, position, and direction.
  3. 3Drag the curtain with the mouse or touch to bend the screen by hand.

Fan control

Move the wind

The fan can move left and right, up and down, or forward and backward. Reset brings it back to the default cinema position.

← → Move across the curtain
↓ ↑ Raise or lower the fan
↙ ↗ Push it back or forward

3D axis handles

Drag the red, green, and blue 3D arrows attached to the fan to move it on one axis at a time.

Reset position

Use Reset fan if the fan moves out of view or you want to return to the balanced starting point.

Night cinema

Open-air screen

Turn on Night cinema for a romantic star field, then adjust light direction, size, strength, range, and star glow.

Wheel zooms only the camera view. Content scroll moves only the instruction page. Upload again any time to replace the curtain texture.
